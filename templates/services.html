Our approach

From first thought
to final detail.

Every project begins with its own brief. Our approach is to understand it clearly, give the work a considered direction, and keep the different elements connected as the project develops.

  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Clarify the purpose, priorities and practical needs of the space.

  2. 02

    Shape the direction

    Bring the design intent, materials and key decisions into focus.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the work

    Connect the people, production and on-site activities involved in delivery.

  4. 04

    Resolve the details

    Keep function, finish and the overall vision in view through to completion.

Questions

Before you
get in touch.

Can't find your question? Book a consultation and we will talk it through.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Construction management, interior design and furniture production can each be taken on their own. Many projects begin with one and add the others as they develop.

Where do you work?

Our projects are in Abuja and Lagos. If your project is elsewhere, tell us about it and we will discuss what is possible.

Do you make furniture for spaces you did not design?

Yes. We can produce pieces to your own drawings or specifications, or help develop the design with you.

Can I see examples of your work?

The portfolio shows offices, residences, shops and hospitality spaces, with photographs for every project. You can open each one to see the full gallery.

How do we get started?

Book a consultation. We will ask about the space, the brief and your timeline, then agree the next step together.
