Our perspective

Three disciplines.
One connected vision.

A well-considered space is shaped by more than one decision. It takes a clear direction, thoughtful design and careful execution.

At Verrazzano & Grit, Construction Management, Interior Design and Furniture Production are connected parts of the same conversation. Each brings a different perspective to a project, helping the details work together from the initial brief through to the finished environment.

Our focus is on understanding what a space needs to do, then bringing its practical and visual elements into a coherent whole.
